### Learnings
- A `Skill` or `Write` call that fails on a missing or unexpected parameter just needs a `ToolSearch select:<Tool>` before retrying. Calling `ToolSearch` up front avoids the wasted round trip.
- `~` inside double quotes is not expanded by the shell. Example commands given to the user should use `$HOME`, an unquoted `~`, or the project-relative `.claude/skills/...` path.
- A trailing space in a directory name breaks tool paths silently. When "directory does not exist" appears for a path that was just listed, check the exact name.
- The warning above does not change results. It appears on every `uv run` here and adds noise to stderr.
- The datasets are fully synthetic. Metrics like the 11% CBK rate, the maturity curves and the wallet/card differences come from generated data, not real fraud behaviour.
